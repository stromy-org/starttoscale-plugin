Motif — Step Rail

Divider
Card accent
Diagnose
Finance functie

Micro / bulletKPI-rij

Construction

  • Four bars, equal width, equal gap.
  • Heights step +18% each — a fixed geometric ascent.
  • 1px corner radius; never rounded into "bar chart" cuteness.
  • Amber on navy (hero) · navy on paper (marks) · single-colour always.
  • Micro form (16–18px) keeps all four bars — the ascent must survive.

Meaning

  • Start → scale, literal stepped ascent.
  • Doubles as the symbol mark, the divider, and a data-bar language.
